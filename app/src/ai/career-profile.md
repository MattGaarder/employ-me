# Career Narrative

My career has developed around three recurring areas:

**Learning → Technology → Creative Communication**

I began with film production and teaching, developing an understanding of visual storytelling, communication, and how people learn.

My experience teaching in Japan developed my ability to create learning materials, explain concepts clearly, organise learning activities, and work across cultures.

I subsequently moved into localisation QA and AI-related data work, developing strong attention to detail, quality assurance, structured communication, and technical problem-solving.

I then moved more directly into digital learning and eLearning development at PwC, where I combined instructional design, multimedia production, Articulate Storyline, Adobe Creative Suite, JavaScript, AI, and automation.

Alongside my professional learning-development career, I developed formal web-development skills through a front-end development bootcamp and continued building technical experience.

My current AI data-contribution work extends this technical trajectory into AI training and coding.

The common thread is my ability to **translate complex ideas into clear experiences while using technology to make those experiences more effective**.
